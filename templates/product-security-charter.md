## Mission and business outcomes

State the capabilities, customers and information the program protects. Explain how it supports safe delivery and change.

## Scope and boundaries

Define customer-facing products, internal applications, integrations, development systems and vendor dependencies in scope. Identify adjacent functions and handoffs. Explain who owns purchased-platform configuration and custom code.

## Principles

Start with business consequences. Build security into requirements and defaults. Make consequential trust decisions explicit. Require evidence proportionate to the claim. Improve recurring causes of failure.

## Responsibilities and decision rights

| Function or actual role | Accountabilities | Decisions / escalation | Named owner or assignment gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| CISO or designated security executive | Strategy, requirements, independent assessment and escalation | Propose standards and escalate material exposure | |
| Product Security | Specialist guidance, design review, testing coordination and verification | Advise and assess against agreed requirements | |
| Engineering | Implementation, remediation and operation of owned software | Delivery within approved criteria | |
| Product / business owner | Business requirements, prioritization and outcome ownership | Scope and priority decisions within authority | |
| IT / platform / vendors | Defined access, platform and service responsibilities | Per agreed system ownership | |
| Authorized business risk owner | Resources and residual-risk decisions | Time-bounded acceptance within delegated authority | |

Adapt roles to existing staffing. Do not imply that each function needs its own department.

## Operating commitments

Describe intake, design review, delivery checks, findings management, maintenance and incident handoffs. Set resource commitments and review cadence with accountable leaders. Mark all proposed commitments as pending until approved.

## Release and exception decisions

Define agreed release criteria, escalation for material exposure, designated decision authority and exception expiry. Record scope, impact, compensating measures, accountable risk owner, correction date and verification. Security advice and business risk acceptance have distinct owners.

## Evidence and measures

Define what demonstrates implementation and what demonstrates effectiveness. Choose measures tied to critical workflows, unresolved exposure, remediation and recurrence. Establish baselines before inventing targets.

## Initial priorities

Tie a short action list to the business profile. Give each item an owner, resource dependency and completion condition.

## Approval and review

Record unresolved decisions. Retain Proposed status until actual approval. Revisit after material changes and on the agreed schedule.
